Privacy

Short version: this site keeps almost nothing about you.

What is stored on your device

The interactive library saves your starred links, notes on reading progress, and view preferences (such as sort order and theme) in your browser's local storage. That data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What we collect

This site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views and clicks on outbound links, and to record what people type in the search box (the words only, not linked to you). GoatCounter says it collects no personally identifiable information and stores only aggregate data, not your IP address or browser headers, and that it probably does not require a consent notice, so none is shown. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and requested page) for security and operations.

Affiliate links

If you click an affiliate link (Amazon), that company may set cookies and record that you came from this site, under its own privacy policy. This site does not receive that information.

Third-party content

Thumbnails and embedded videos load from the original sites (for example YouTube, GitHub and creators' own sites). Those sites may set their own cookies or log your visit, under their own policies. Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed.

Newsletter

There is no newsletter yet. When there is, signing up will ask only for your email address, use double opt-in, include an unsubscribe link in every issue, and never be sold or shared.
