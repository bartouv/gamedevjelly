Vertex displacement & animation

Moving vertices in the shader lets you animate meshes for free on the GPU: waving grass, flags, wobbling jelly, melting and morphing. This section explains displacement, vertex colours as animation masks and how to keep normals and shadows correct after the mesh has moved. 26 hand-picked Unity guides, tutorials, videos and repos, kept current.
