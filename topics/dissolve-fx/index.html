Dissolve, holograms & sci-fi FX

Dissolves, holograms, glitches, force fields and other sci-fi effects mostly come from a handful of shader ideas: noise thresholds, fresnel and scanlines. These guides show how to build them and how to animate them from gameplay code. 29 hand-picked Unity guides, tutorials, videos and repos, kept current.
