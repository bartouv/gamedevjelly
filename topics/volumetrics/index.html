Fog, light shafts, clouds and atmospheric scattering add depth that flat lighting cannot. This section covers raymarched volumes, height fog, god rays and volumetric lighting in Unity. These effects are costly, so many guides also show how to fake them cheaply. 40 hand-picked Unity guides, tutorials, videos and repos, kept current.
