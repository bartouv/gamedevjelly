Raymarching & SDF

Raymarching with signed distance fields renders shapes from maths alone, with soft shadows, blends and fractals for almost free. These guides cover SDF primitives, operators, lighting and how to use raymarching inside Unity shaders. It is mind-bending the first time and very powerful afterwards. 45 hand-picked Unity guides, tutorials, videos and repos, kept current.
