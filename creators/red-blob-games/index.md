# Red Blob Games

Amit Patel’s site of interactive tutorials on hex grids, A* pathfinding and procedural map generation. Engine-agnostic and widely used as the reference by game developers, with code samples in several languages including C#.

- site: https://www.redblobgames.com/
- x: https://x.com/redblobgames

- [Mapgen4: procedural map generator](https://www.redblobgames.com/maps/mapgen4/): Painted-mountain map generator with rainfall, rivers and erosion, plus a write-up of how it works. (Red Blob Games, 2018-07-20, article, Unity-old era)
- [Resource placement on a map](https://www.redblobgames.com/x/1736-resource-placement/): Placing trees, ore and other resources with blue noise and spacing rules instead of pure random. (Red Blob Games, 2017-09-09, article, Unity-old era)
- [Mapgen2: procedural island map generator](https://www.redblobgames.com/maps/mapgen2/): Voronoi-based island generator with elevation, rivers, moisture and biomes, plus source code. (Red Blob Games, 2017-09-01, article, Unity-old era)
- [Making maps with noise functions](https://www.redblobgames.com/maps/terrain-from-noise/): Turn noise into islands, biomes and terrain, with octaves, redistribution and moisture explained. (Red Blob Games, 2015-07-07, article, Unity-old era)
- [Implementation of Hex Grids (with Unity/C# code)](https://www.redblobgames.com/grids/hexagons/implementation.html): Copy-ready hex grid code in C# and other languages, covering storage, maps and rendering. (Red Blob Games, 2015-05-06, article, Unity-old era)
- [Introduction to the A* Algorithm](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/a-star/introduction.html): Interactive walkthrough from breadth-first search to Dijkstra to A*, with heuristics explained. (Red Blob Games, 2014-05-26, article, Unity-old era)
- [Implementation of A*](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/a-star/implementation.html): Python, C++ and C# implementations of A* with priority queues and path reconstruction. (Red Blob Games, 2014-05-26, article, Unity-old era)
- [Breadth First Search: multiple start points](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/distance-to-any/): Distance maps from many seed points, useful for influence maps, spawn rules and AI queries. (Red Blob Games, 2014-04-14, article, Unity-old era)
- [Grid pathfinding optimizations](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/grids/algorithms.html): Faster pathfinding on grids: jump points, hierarchical maps and other speed-ups, with their trade-offs. (Red Blob Games, 2014-03-02, article, Unity-old era)
- [Flow Field Pathfinding for Tower Defense](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/tower-defense/): One search from the goal gives every unit its direction, ideal for crowds and tower defence. (Red Blob Games, 2014-02-06, article, Unity-old era)
- [Hexagonal Grids: coordinates, neighbours, distances and conversions](https://www.redblobgames.com/grids/hexagons/): The reference guide to hex grids: coordinate systems, neighbours, distance, rotation, rings and pixel conversion. (Red Blob Games, 2013-03-11, article, Unity-old era)
- [Grids and Graphs](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/grids/graphs.html): How to model a game map as a graph, and when grids, meshes or waypoints suit pathfinding. (Red Blob Games, 2013-02-15, article, Unity-old era)
