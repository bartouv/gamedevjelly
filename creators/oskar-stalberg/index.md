# Oskar Stålberg

Swedish indie developer behind Bad North and Townscaper, known for talks on Wave Function Collapse, irregular grids and tile-based procedural generation.

- site: https://oskarstalberg.com
- x: https://x.com/OskSta
- bluesky: https://bsky.app/profile/oskarstalberg.bsky.social

- [Oskar Stålberg: Landscapes of Hex and Square - Summer School on AI and Games 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NpfoRAzfGDg): How to build procedural content systems on hex and square grids, from Bad North to Townscaper. (Oskar Stålberg, 2025-02-26, video, Unity-6 era)
- [Hierarchical AI state system from Bad North (thread)](https://bsky.app/profile/oskarstalberg.bsky.social/post/3lbekwegpfc2v): Thread on Bad North's nested state hierarchy AI, its cache-miss costs and a source-generated alternative. (Oskar Stålberg, 2024-11-20, post, Unity-6 era)
- [Oskar Stålberg- Townscaper- A tour of Townscaper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sBMnM2-lOPs): Tour of Townscaper's technology, grid design and aesthetics, presented to an architecture audience. (Oskar Stålberg, 2022-08-17, video, Unity-2022 era)
- [Konsoll 2021: Oskar Stålberg - The Story of Townscaper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xrRTOikBBg): How Townscaper was built: irregular quad grid, WFC-driven tiles and the design decisions around them. (Oskar Stålberg, 2022-03-16, video, Unity-2022 era)
- [SGC21- Oskar Stålberg - Beyond Townscapers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uxeo9c-PX-w): Experimental tile-based procedural generation beyond Townscaper: irregular grids, dual meshes and new tile setups. (Oskar Stålberg, 2021-12-08, video, Unity-2021 era)
- [EPC2021 | Q&A session with Oskar Stålberg | Oskar Stålberg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NOJYZYqY6_M): Q&A under Townscaper's hood: Marching Cubes plus WFC on an irregular quadrilateral grid at interactive speed. (Oskar Stålberg, 2021-07-15, video, Unity-2021 era)
- [ORGANIC TOWNS FROM SQUARE TILES - a talk by OSKAR STÅLBERG at INDIECADE EUROPE 2019](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1hqt8JkYRdI): Generating cozy towns from cube tiles by combining Marching Cubes with Wave Function Collapse. (Oskar Stålberg, 2020-03-17, video, Unity-old era)
- [Konsoll 2018: Oskar Stålberg - Developing The Bad North Look](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6JcFbivo8dQ): Procedural techniques, technical challenges and art reasoning behind Bad North's minimalist island look. (Oskar Stålberg, 2019-01-29, video, Unity-old era)
- [Tech Toolbox (GDC 2018) – includes Oskar Stålberg](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1025002/Tech): Short GDC tool talks; Stålberg's segment covers his tile-based Wave Function Collapse workflow. (Oskar Stålberg, 2018-03-19, article, Unity-old era)
- [Polygonal Planet Project – a study in tilesets](https://oskarstalberg.tumblr.com/post/136461533291/polygonal-planet-project-a-study-in-tilesets-by): Playable browser study of marching-style tilesets wrapped around a polygonal planet surface. (Oskar Stålberg, 2016-01-01, article, Unity-old era)
