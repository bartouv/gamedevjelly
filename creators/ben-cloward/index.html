Ben Cloward

Shader creation tutorials for Unity and Unreal, from a long-time game developer who co-created the ShaderFX node-based shader tool and has worked on the Shader Graph team at Unity. His Shader Graph Basics series runs to more than 60 episodes, from your first shader to toon looks and post-processing.
