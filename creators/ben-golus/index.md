# Ben Golus

Shader and graphics programmer who writes long, careful articles on shader technique: anti-aliased alpha test, triplanar normal mapping, mipmapping, outlines, grids and normal maps. Mostly Unity, and useful well beyond it.

- medium: https://bgolus.medium.com/
- github: https://gist.github.com/bgolus
- kofi: https://ko-fi.com/bgolus
- x: https://x.com/bgolus

- [The Best Darn Grid Shader (Yet)](https://bgolus.medium.com/the-best-darn-grid-shader-yet-727f9278b9d8): How to draw a clean, anti-aliased grid in a shader that holds up at any distance and viewing angle. (Ben Golus, 2023-10-20, article, Unity-2022 era)
- [Showing multiple matcap techniques, including a proposed improved method that's no more expensive than the usual fix if you're starting with the world position and world normal.](https://gist.github.com/bgolus/02e37cd76568520e20219dc51653ceaa): Gist: Compares several matcap techniques including an improved method. (Ben Golus, 2022-01-29, article, Unity-2022 era)
- [Distinctive Derivative Differences](https://bgolus.medium.com/distinctive-derivative-differences-cce38d36797b): A look at the different kinds of shader derivatives and how they differ in practice. (Ben Golus, 2021-04-13, article, Unity-2021 era)
- [Different methods for getting World Normal from Depth Texture, without any external script dependencies.](https://gist.github.com/bgolus/a07ed65602c009d5e2f753826e8078a0): Repo: shader gist with several ways to reconstruct world-space normals from the depth texture without scripts. (Ben Golus, 2021-02-26, article, Unity-2021 era)
- [Rendering a Sphere on a Quad](https://bgolus.medium.com/rendering-a-sphere-on-a-quad-13c92025570c): Ben Golus explains rendering a sphere impostor on a quad and improving how convincing it looks. (Ben Golus, 2021-01-10, article, Unity-2021 era)
- [Progressing in Circles](https://medium.com/@bgolus/progressing-in-circles-13452434fdb9): Medium post by Ben Golus on progressing in circles, a shader or math topic around circular shapes. (Ben Golus, 2020-06-01, article, Unity-old era)
- [Generating Perfect Normal Maps for Unity (and Other Programs)](https://bgolus.medium.com/generating-perfect-normal-maps-for-unity-f929e673fc57): How to generate accurate normal maps for Unity and other programs from a high-poly model. (Ben Golus, 2020-02-03, article, Unity-old era)
- [Sharper Mipmapping using Shader Based Supersampling](https://bgolus.medium.com/sharper-mipmapping-using-shader-based-supersampling-ed7aadb47bec): A shader-based supersampling approach for sharper results than plain mipmapping. (Ben Golus, 2019-08-10, article, Unity-old era)
- [The Team Color Problem](https://bgolus.medium.com/the-team-color-problem-b70ec69d109f): Approaches to recolouring part of an object, such as team colours, in a shader. (Ben Golus, 2018-07-11, article, Unity-old era)
- [Normal Mapping for a Triplanar Shader](https://bgolus.medium.com/normal-mapping-for-a-triplanar-shader-10bf39dca05a): How to get correct normal mapping on triplanar-mapped surfaces. (Ben Golus, 2017-09-17, article, Unity-old era)
- [Anti-aliased Alpha Test: The Esoteric Alpha To Coverage](https://bgolus.medium.com/anti-aliased-alpha-test-the-esoteric-alpha-to-coverage-8b177335ae4f): How alpha to coverage gives anti-aliased alpha-tested edges, which matters most in VR. (Ben Golus, 2017-08-12, article, Unity-old era)
- [The Quest for Very Wide Outlines](https://bgolus.medium.com/the-quest-for-very-wide-outlines-ba82ed442cd9): Ben Golus explores GPU silhouette rendering for very wide outlines. (Ben Golus, article)
