# Team Dogpit

Small game studio whose graphics programmer Xibanya (Manuela Malasaña) wrote the 'Shaders For People Who Don't Know How To Shader' beginner series for Unity.

- site: https://xibanya.github.io/ShaderTutorials/

- [Shaders For People Who Don't Know How To Shader - Part 12: Dissolve](https://xibanya.github.io/ShaderTutorials/tutorial/Part12Dissolve.html): Builds a dissolve shader on top of the cutout technique from the previous part. (Team Dogpit, 2019-11-07, article, Unity-old era)
- [GitHub - Xibanya/ShaderTutorials: Shaders For People Who Don't Know How To Shader, a tutorial series for beginners!](https://github.com/Xibanya/ShaderTutorials): Repo: beginner-friendly shader tutorial series. (Team Dogpit, 2019-08-17, repo, Unity-old era)
- [Shaders For People Who Don't Know How To Shader - Part 11: Cutouts](https://xibanya.github.io/ShaderTutorials/tutorial/Part11Cutouts.html): Builds a cutout shader that discards pixels based on a texture in a beginner shader series. (Team Dogpit, 2019-07-11, article, Unity-old era)
- [Shaders For People Who Don't Know How To Shader - Part 10: Toon Lighting](https://xibanya.github.io/ShaderTutorials/tutorial/Part10ToonLighting.html): Builds a toon lighting shader step by step in a beginner-friendly shader series. (Team Dogpit, 2019-07-09, article, Unity-old era)
