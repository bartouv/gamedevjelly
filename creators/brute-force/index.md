# Brute Force Studio

Solo developer in France making Paper Sky and Crumble in Unity. Wrote a 2019 devblog on the shaders and mechanics behind Crumble, and sells the stylized shaders (sand, grass, snow and ice, ground) on the Asset Store.

- site: https://www.bruteforcegame.com/
- x: https://x.com/BruteForceGame

- [Custom sand shader with player trails, from Paper Sky](https://80.lv/articles/check-out-a-custom-sand-shader-created-in-unity-for-a-game): A showcase of the sand shader behind the Paper Sky desert level: a trail follows the player through the sand. (Brute Force Studio, 2024-02-01, article, Unity-2022 era)
- [Tongue tether swing mechanic in 3D (Devblog #14)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/tongue-tether-swing-3d-devblog-14): A grappling-hook style swing built for a 3D platformer. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-11-17, article, Unity-old era)
- [Watercolor shader (Devblog #13)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/watercolor-shader-devblog-13): A Moebius-inspired watercolor look: cutout texture, outline, unlit coloured shadow, moving textures and an ink mask. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-10-20, article, Unity-old era)
- [Curved world and sea water shader (Devblog #11)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/curved-water-sea-shader-devblog-11): A vertex-shader world curvature effect, like in endless-runner mobile games, plus a flat-style sea. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-08-22, article, Unity-old era)
- [How to make a rolling ball feel juicy and responsive (Devblog #9)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/how-to-roll-a-ball-devlog-09): Taking the standard roll-a-ball setup further with tuned movement and input response. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-07-14, article, Unity-old era)
- [Image effects shader basics (Devblog #8)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/image-effects-shader-devblog-08): How post-process shaders run from an OnRenderImage script, and how to chain several effects. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-07-07, article, Unity-old era)
- [Vertical ground fog shader (Devblog #7)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/vertical-fog-shader-devblog-07): Ground fog that fades by height, built from a mesh and used as a gameplay boundary. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-05-17, article, Unity-old era)
- [Sky and fog shader with flat clouds (Devblog #6)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/sky-and-fog-shader-devblog-06): A moving-cloud skybox shader and the fog that goes with it. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-05-10, article, Unity-old era)
- [Flat shader and character animation for a game jam (Devblog #5)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/sheep-it-shader-devblog-05): Two shaders behind a flat, cartoon look made during a Ludum Dare jam. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-05-04, article, Unity-old era)
- [Grass shader that bends as the player rolls through it (Devblog #4)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/grass-shader-devblog-04): Dense interactive grass that blends with the terrain, without placing thousands of objects by hand. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-04-26, article, Unity-old era)
- [Physics settings and optimization for a physics-driven game (Devblog #3)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/physic-and-optimization-devblog-03): Gravity, fixed timestep and other physics settings that decide how a game feels, and how to keep them cheap. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-04-15, article, Unity-old era)
- [Soft-body jelly shader for a squishy character (Devblog #2)](https://www.bruteforcegame.com/post/soft-body-shader-jelly-joy-devblog-2): Fake a squishy soft body in real time with a vertex shader instead of a physics simulation. (Brute Force Studio, 2019-04-12, article, Unity-old era)
